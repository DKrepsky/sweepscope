# M3 hardware checklist (run by the human on the Hantek 6254BC)

Closes the M3 behaviour that the simulator cannot prove. Spec: [implementation-spec.md](implementation-spec.md). Agents never touch the scope.
Record pass or fail with a word of context per item; keep measured figures in your own notes (ADR 0008), the QA report carries outcomes.
Setup unless stated: CH1 probe on the `CAL` terminal (1 kHz square, about 1.9 V peak to peak), 1x probe, CH1 only, DC. Run `RUST_LOG=info` in the terminal. Use your real display session, not Xvfb.

| ID | Do | Expect |
|---|---|---|
| H-01 | Time base 500 ms/div (roll), CH1 only, then 4 channels (CAL on CH1, others open). Watch for two minutes. Try Trigger, Single, Force, XY, FFT, Persist, Math, Cursors. | Trace enters at the right edge and scrolls left; about 500 samples per second per channel in the log; every listed control is disabled and states why; Record is enabled. Repeat at 2 s/div: slower scroll, no stall. |
| H-02 | Roll at 500 ms/div with 4 channels (RAM span about 33 s). Stop the app process with `kill -STOP <pid>` for 45 s, then `kill -CONT`. Repeat once with 1 channel and a 140 s stop. | A gap band with `GAP` and an approximate length appears where the stop happened, the trace continues after it, `Gaps 1` shows in the status bar, no crash and no silent splice. If the pause is shorter than the span no gap is expected. First hardware proof of `Overrun`: note whether the reported size is plausible. |
| H-03 | Roll at 500 ms/div, 4 channels, Record to a file, for one hour. Read RSS of the app every 10 minutes after the first 10 (`ps -o rss`). Then repeat one run with the window showing no canvas (blank page build or a hidden screen as the spike report describes). | File grows linearly (about 20 KB per minute for 4 channels); display never stalls; Rust RSS grows at most 2 MiB over the hour measured after the ring is full, or the growth is attributed to the UI process (spike #4 condition). The file opens with `tools/roll-record-reader.py`, gaps marked. |
| H-04 | Record to a 1 MB tmpfs (`sudo mount -t tmpfs -o size=1m tmpfs /mnt/small`), then to a normal folder and delete the file while recording. | Disk full: recording stops, error Banner with the reason and bytes written, the display keeps rolling. Deleted file: recording stops with `the file was removed` within about 2 s. Both Banners offer Choose new file and Dismiss. |
| H-05 | Single at 200 us/div with the trigger level above the CAL signal, then lower the level. Trigger position 10 %, 50 %, 90 %, with 1, 2 and 4 channels. Export raw each time and measure the edge index in the data (reader snippet in the format document). Also try Stop while armed and Single again. | `Waiting for trigger` while armed, one frame, `Stop` and `Done`, frame stays until Run or Single. The rising edge is within 8 samples of the chosen position for all nine cases (driver measured +6 to -6). Stop cancels at once on screen. Note any case that fails by how many samples. |
| H-06 | Pulse trigger on CAL (500 us high, 1 kHz) at 200 us/div, positive polarity: `>` 400 us, `>` 600 us, `<` 600 us, `<` 400 us, `=` 500 us, `=` 480 us, `=` 520 us, `=` 450 us, `≠` 200 us, `≠` 500 us. Then negative polarity with `=` 500 us (the low part is 500 us). Enter `abc` as width. | Triggers (stable picture) for `>` 400, `<` 600, `=` 500, 480, 520, `≠` 200; waits (`Waiting for trigger`) for `>` 600, `<` 400, `=` 450, `≠` 500. `abc` shows the error and keeps the applied width. Note the `≠` result at 480 and 520 us (assumed complement of the 5 % window). |
| H-07 | Auto-setup on CAL from three starting setups (1 ms/div and 5 V/div; 2 ns/div and 2 mV/div; 200 ms/div and 10 V/div). Then short the input (probe tip to ground clip) and press Auto-setup; then leave the probe open and press it. Time each run. | CAL: about 200 us or 500 us per division and 500 mV/div (3.8 divisions), 2 to 6 periods, trigger stable, Toast with the values; run time under 10 s. Shorted: flat line at zero, `No signal` tag and Toast; the tag clears when a signal appears. Open input: note what happens (PRD 5.2: pickup is not zero). No dialog at any point. |
| H-08 | FFT on CAL at 200 us/div, CH1 only; Hann, then Rectangular, Hamming, Blackman; dBV and Linear; Auto peak, then Manual click on the third harmonic; use two channels (CAL on CH1 and CH2). | Peak at 1.0 kHz within one bin; odd harmonics at 3 and 5 kHz, even ones far lower; bin spacing text equals sample rate / 4096 (305 Hz at 1.25 MS/s; read the sample rate from the status bar); peak tag, panel and marker agree; two channels show two coloured spectra. |
| H-09 | CAL on CH1, CH1 only, time base 20 ms/div, then 100 ms/div, then 200 ms/div. | No `Alias` at 20 ms/div (Fs/5 is 2.5 kHz); `Alias CH1` badge in the status bar, scale chip and measurement header appears at 100 ms/div and 200 ms/div (1 kHz above Fs/5 = 500 Hz and 250 Hz) with the tooltip naming the faster time base; it clears together when the time base returns to 20 ms/div. |
| H-10 | CAL on CH1 and CH2 (two probes), 200 us/div. Math A-B (flat near zero), A+B (twice the level), Invert A; set CH2 to 200 mV/div for the Mixed badge; switch CH2 off. XY CH1 against CH2; swap axes; back to Yt. Persistence 1 s, touch the probe tip with a finger to create a glitch, Infinite, Clear. | Math traces have their own scale and unit in row, chip and badge; CH2 off shows `Unavailable` with the reason; XY shows a diagonal line for equal signals, axes swap works, returning to Yt leaves every setting intact; persistence shows ghosts that fade over 1 s and a glitch that stays visible, cursors still work, Clear empties it. |
